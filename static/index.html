How it works

1

Your agent calls a paid endpoint without payment headers.

2

Server responds 402 Payment Required with the price (in USDC) and the payTo address.

3

Agent signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization and re-sends with X-PAYMENT.

4

Facilitator settles on Base. Server returns the data.

Source & freshness

SSB tables are queried on-demand and cached for 24 h (registry data is monthly/quarterly). Norges Bank exchange rates are cached for 1 h, the policy rate for 6 h. Each response carries an X-Cache: HIT|MISS header so callers know freshness.

Coverage: ~50 Norwegian municipalities (top by population, post-2024 reform codes); ~40 currencies tracked by Norges Bank.
